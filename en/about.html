about

who I am,
how I work

I'm Ali Tunç, a system and network administrator. I work full-time for an international manufacturer. I manage, maintain and improve the infrastructure there and take part in IT projects. IT at one of the sites is my responsibility. Now and then we do configuration and maintenance on location at sites abroad.

Recent work: moved laptops to a new domain with Hybrid Entra ID join, rolled out an EDR agent to the servers, ran and documented restore tests, fitted ISP routers and a core switch in behind the firewall, and analysed connection problems with cloud telephony for more than a hundred users on the firewall side.

I have a few fixed habits. I write down what I do, in runbooks a colleague can follow. I test before I roll anything out. And a backup only counts once the restore has worked.

Security is part of the daily work: hardening, segmentation, firewall policy, EDR. In an isolated lab I test attacks and defences. What I see there goes back into how I run things.
